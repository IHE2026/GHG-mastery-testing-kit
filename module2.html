Module 2: GHG Protocol Essentials

Beginner to Intermediate

Learning Objectives

  • Understand what the GHG Protocol is.
  • Explain Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3.
  • Understand organisational boundaries.
  • Classify emissions correctly.

Lesson 1: What is the GHG Protocol?

The GHG Protocol is the most widely used global framework for measuring and managing greenhouse gas emissions. It helps organisations calculate, classify, and report emissions consistently.

Lesson 2: The Three Scopes

  • Scope 1: Direct emissions from owned or controlled sources.
  • Scope 2: Indirect emissions from purchased electricity, heat, or steam.
  • Scope 3: All other indirect emissions across the value chain.

Lesson 3: Organisational Boundaries

Before calculating emissions, an organisation must define what parts of the business are included. This can be based on control or equity share, depending on the reporting approach.

Classification Exercise

Classify each item into Scope 1, 2, or 3:

  • Diesel used in company vehicles
  • Electricity bought for the office
  • Business flights taken by staff

Suggested answers: Scope 1, Scope 2, Scope 3

Short Assessment

Write a short paragraph explaining the difference between Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 using one example for each.
